About

AI has come so far — so why can't it be put to work right away on task-specific problems? I study the gap between theory and practice by looking at each problem the way domain experts do — so the AI I build actually helps them.

Task-specific · Clinical AI

Annotator disagreement

Experts often disagree on the same image. Working with a partner fertility clinic, I train models on the full spread of expert opinions so they can flag contested cases and defer to experts.

Methodology

Annotation refinement

When a guideline leaves labels undetermined, experts fill the gaps differently. I use VLM judges to check labels against a rubric, hand unresolved cases to experts, and refine the rubric each round.

Reproductive health

An understudied area in machine learning. I use topic modeling on the women's reproductive health literature to map research trends.
